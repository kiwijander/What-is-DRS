Formula 1 explained

What is DRS in F1? Explained in two minutes

DRS stands for Drag Reduction System. It is a button that lets a driver open a flap on the rear wing to go faster on a straight, so it is easier to pass the car in front.

How DRS works

Three steps on every lap with a DRS zone.

1. Detection

A timing line on the track, just before the DRS zone, measures the gap between a car and the car ahead.

2. Activation

If the gap is one second or less, the driver gets a signal. Once in the zone, the driver presses a button on the steering wheel.

3. Flap opens

The upper flap of the rear wing lifts. Less air pushes back on the car, so the top speed goes up. Braking closes the flap again.
